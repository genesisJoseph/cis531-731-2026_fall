# MP 9 Codebook (v1)

Genesis Joseph, CIS 731. One label per song (BTS, 50 songs). Written after
annotation and adjudication, to describe the rules I used.

| Label | Use it when the song is mainly about... |
|---|---|
| love | being in love, attraction, wanting someone (Boy With Luv, DNA) |
| longing | missing someone, heartbreak, a relationship falling apart (Spring Day) |
| loving oneself | accepting yourself, self-worth, identity (Epiphany, IDOL) |
| generational inequality | pressure from society, class, school, the "silver spoon" gap (Baepsae, N.O) |
| joy | partying, dancing, feel-good escape (Dynamite, Butter) |
| mutual comfort | the bond between BTS and fans or friends supporting each other (Magic Shop) |
| youth | growing up, being young and unsure, running toward the future (RUN, Young Forever) |
| artistic passion | music career, ambition, fear of losing passion for the art (Black Swan, ON) |

## Tie-break rules (added after adjudication)
1. **Heartbreak beats romance.** If the relationship is failing, painful or fake,
   use *longing*, not *love* (d006 I NEED U, d011 FAKE LOVE).
2. **Message beats beat.** An upbeat song whose lyrics are about self-acceptance is
   *loving oneself*, not *joy* (d012 IDOL).
3. **Pure fun stays joy.** If there is no deeper message than dancing and good
   vibes, use *joy* even if it mentions love (d014 Butter).
4. If two labels still fit, pick the one the chorus is about.

## Sources used while labeling
Wikipedia, BTS Wiki (Fandom), ibighit.com, and my own knowledge of the songs.
